Pregonero

A personal desktop tool for preparing and scheduling videos.

What it is

Pregonero is a small application that runs only on its author's own computer. It helps the author prepare short videos, write their titles and descriptions, and schedule them to be uploaded to the author's own accounts on YouTube and TikTok, using the official APIs of each platform.

What it is not

  • It is not a public service and it has no user accounts or sign-up.
  • It does not run on a server and does not collect data about other people.
  • It does not publish anything on its own: every upload is confirmed by the person who runs it.
