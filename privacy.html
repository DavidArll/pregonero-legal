Privacy Policy

Pregonero. Last updated: November 2026.

Pregonero is a personal application that runs only on its author's own computer. It is not a hosted service. This policy explains what information the application handles and where that information stays.

1. Who is responsible

The author of Pregonero, who is also its only user. Contact: alx.arellano@gmail.com.

2. Information the application handles

  • Access to the author's own accounts. When the author connects a YouTube or TikTok account, the platform gives the application an access token through its official OAuth sign-in. The application never sees or stores the account password.
  • Basic account details. The name and identifier of the connected account, so the application can show which account is connected.
  • Content prepared by the author. Video files, titles, descriptions, hashtags, schedule times and comments that the author writes inside the application.

3. Where it is stored

All of this information is stored on the author's computer. Access tokens are stored encrypted. Nothing is sent to the author's servers, because there are none. The application has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

4. How it is used and shared

The information is used only to upload and schedule the author's own videos to the platform the author chooses. Video files and text are sent only to that platform, through its official API. The information is not sold, shared with third parties or used for any other purpose.

5. YouTube API Services

Pregonero uses YouTube API Services. By using it with a YouTube account, the author is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to Pregonero can be revoked at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

6. TikTok

Pregonero uses the TikTok for Developers APIs. Access granted to Pregonero can be removed at any time from the app and security settings of the TikTok account.

7. Retention and deletion

Information stays on the computer until the author deletes it. The application includes an option to revoke access and delete the stored credentials. Deleting the application's data folder removes everything it stored.

8. Children

Pregonero is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information about them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: alx.arellano@gmail.com.
